Rut IQ privacy notice

Updated October 9, 2026 · Ghost Ridge Brand LLC

Information used by Rut IQ

Rut IQ uses your email and sign-in session to manage your hunter account. It stores your membership status and submitted observations: state, county, behavior, observation date, submission time and optional notes.

What other hunters see

County activity totals, behavior summaries and eligible report details are shared according to membership access. Public reports do not display your email, account identifier or notes. Do not put a stand location, property address or other private information in a report.

Maps and searches

Rut IQ does not request your device’s GPS location. A map search is sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service; that provider receives the text you search and normal connection information. Search markers remain in your current map session and are not included in hunter reports. You can search a city or ZIP instead of an exact address.

Map tiles are loaded from Esri or OpenStreetMap, and map software from unpkg. These services receive normal web requests, including your IP address and requested resources. Supabase provides account and database services; Render hosts this app. Those providers may process operational logs.

Storage on your device

Your selected region and sign-in session are saved in browser storage. Sign out on shared devices. Clearing browser site data removes the saved session and region from that browser, but does not delete your server-side account or observations.

Requests and retention

Account and report data remain available to operate Rut IQ until removed through support or service administration. Provider backups and operational logs may persist separately. Contact Ghost Ridge to ask about access, correction, deletion or retention of your information.

Rut IQ accounts and memberships are separate from Woods IQ. Paid checkout is currently disabled; this app does not collect card details.

Rut IQ terms of use

Updated October 9, 2026 · Ghost Ridge Brand LLC

Use real observations

Report only deer activity you actually observed, with the correct county, date and behavior. Do not submit fabricated observations, spam, harassment, private locations or another person’s personal information. Reports that violate these rules may be removed or restricted.

Understand the information

Rut IQ summarizes community observations. Report volume is not a verified measure of deer abundance or rut intensity. No reports does not mean no activity. Coverage depends on hunter participation; observations are not guaranteed predictions.

Hunt responsibly

The map does not grant land access or establish property boundaries. Verify permissions, regulations and safe conditions independently before hunting.

Your account and reports

Keep your sign-in link private. Submitted observations may be displayed and summarized at county level to provide Rut IQ’s features. Public views exclude contact details and private notes. Contact support to request correction or removal.

Free and Pro

Submitting observations remains free. Pro is listed at $7.99 per month or $49.99 per year, but paid checkout is not active. Authorized Pro testers can receive access without a charge. Planned features are labeled and may change as development continues. Woods IQ subscriptions do not automatically include Rut IQ.

Preview availability

Rut IQ is in development. Features and availability may change, and service interruptions can occur. Contact Ghost Ridge with questions or account requests.
