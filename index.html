Your privacy

Rut IQ by Ghost Ridge Brand LLC · Updated October 9, 2026

Your hunting spots stay private from other hunters. Rut IQ shares county-level activity. Your exact hunting location, stand, name and email are not shown publicly.

What we keep

We keep your email so you can sign in, whether you have Free or Pro access, and the reports you submit. A report includes its county, state, deer behavior, observation date, when you sent it and any notes you added.

What other hunters see

Hunters can see county report totals and the activity details included in their plan. Your email, account number and notes aren’t shown publicly. Please don’t put exact hunting spots or someone else’s personal information in a report.

Your hunting spots

Rut IQ does not request or track your phone’s GPS location. We do not use an exact hunting location to create public reports or share one with other hunters. You only need to choose a state and county to report activity.

Map search is optional. The words or address you enter are sent to an outside map service to find that place. Use a town or ZIP code if you don’t want to send an exact address. Your search marker stays in your current map session and is not added to hunter reports or shown to other hunters.

What stays on your phone

Your browser remembers your chosen county and sign-in. Sign out if you share your device. Clearing the browser’s saved data signs you out, but does not delete your account.

Keeping the app running

Companies that help us run accounts, reports and maps process the information needed to provide those features. They may keep basic connection records, such as your internet address. This does not make your hunting spots visible to other hunters.

You’re in control

Go to More → My account → Delete my account to permanently remove your Rut IQ account, membership and submitted reports. You don’t need to contact us. This also removes those reports from future county totals when the data refreshes.

Backup copies and technical records kept by our providers may remain for a time after deletion. Deleting Rut IQ does not delete your Woods IQ or Ghost Ridge store account.

Until you delete them, we keep your account and reports to run Rut IQ. For a copy of your information, a correction or a privacy question, contact us through Help.

The ground rules

Rut IQ terms of use · Ghost Ridge Brand LLC · Updated October 9, 2026

Share what you actually saw. Respect other hunters. Use the reports as one part of your hunt planning.

Keep reports honest

Choose the county and date where you saw the activity. Report what happened—even if you saw no rut activity. Don’t make up reports, post spam or harass anyone. We may remove reports or restrict accounts that break these rules.

Keep hunting spots private

Don’t post stand locations, private property addresses or another hunter’s personal information. Rut IQ shares activity by county, not exact hunting spots.

Reports aren’t promises

A busy county on the map means more reports, not a guaranteed hot spot. A county with no reports may still have deer activity. Rut IQ can’t promise when deer will move, when the rut will peak or whether your hunt will be successful.

Know where you can hunt

The map doesn’t give you permission to enter land or confirm property lines. Check land access, hunting rules and safe conditions for yourself.

Your account and observations

Keep your email sign-in link to yourself. When you submit a report, Rut IQ uses it to show county activity and build summaries for other hunters. Your email and private notes are not displayed publicly.

You can delete your account and all your reports under More → My account. You can contact us to correct or remove an individual report.

Free means you can always contribute

Submitting observations is free. Pro adds deeper activity information at the listed price of $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Checkout is currently off, so you can’t be charged through this preview. Invited Pro testers can use Pro without paying.

Features marked “planned” aren’t available yet. Woods IQ and Rut IQ have separate accounts and memberships.

We’re still building

Rut IQ is a preview. Features may change, and the app may sometimes be unavailable. If something isn’t working, let us know through Help.
